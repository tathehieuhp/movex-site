Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MoveX ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the MoveX website (movex.info.vn, the "Website") and our internal software tools. We are committed to handling data responsibly and transparently.

2. Information Collected via the Website

The Website is a static informational site. We do not use cookies for tracking, do not run analytics scripts, and do not collect personal information from visitors. If you contact us by email, we will use your email address and message content solely to respond to your inquiry.

Chat assistant. The Website offers an optional AI chat assistant. When you use it, the messages you type in that conversation are sent through our server (hosted on Cloudflare) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate replies. We do not store chat conversations, and your IP address is used only transiently to limit request rates. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information in the chat. Anthropic processes the messages under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

3. Data Processed by Our Internal Tools

Our internal tools process data related exclusively to our own e-commerce store and our own advertising accounts, including:

  • Order data of our own store (order IDs, order status, product and shipping information) used for operational tracking;
  • Advertising performance data of advertising accounts that we own (campaign names, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and similar metrics) used for reporting and optimization.

This data is obtained through interfaces provided by the respective platforms, including official APIs such as the TikTok Marketing API, and only for accounts we own or are expressly authorized to manage.

4. How We Use Data

  • Internal operational monitoring and notifications for our own store;
  • Internal reporting and analysis of our own advertising performance;
  • Improving our own campaign settings and budget decisions.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose data to third parties. We do not use data for advertising to, or profiling of, any individuals.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data processed by our internal tools is stored on servers controlled by us, protected by access controls, and accessible only to authorized personnel of MoveX. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

6. Data Retention

We retain operational and advertising data only for as long as it is needed for internal reporting and analysis, after which it may be deleted or anonymized.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Our use of platform APIs (such as the TikTok Marketing API) is subject to the respective platform's terms and privacy policies. We access only the data scopes necessary for the purposes described above and comply with each platform's developer policies, including requirements on data use, storage, and deletion.

8. Your Rights and Contact

If you believe we hold information about you and wish to request access, correction, or deletion, or if you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@movex.info.vn. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
